# FOMC 2026-06-17 决议 + SEP（美联储官方，沃什首次主持）

> 美东 6-17 决议，北京时间 6-18 凌晨公布。WebFetch 声明原文 + SEP 预测表原文（fomcprojtabl20260617.htm）核对。

## 会议结果（声明原文已验证）

- **联邦基金利率**：维持 **3-1/2 to 3-3/4 percent（3.50-3.75%）**（原文 "The Committee decided to maintain the target range for the federal funds rate at 3-1/2 to 3-3/4 percent"）
- **投票**：**12-0 全票通过**，无反对（vs 4-29 的 3 票反对）
- **通胀措辞**："Inflation remains elevated relative to the Committee's 2 percent goal, in part reflecting supply shocks that have driven price increases in certain sectors, including energy. The Committee will deliver price stability."
- **经济措辞**："Economic activity is expanding at a solid pace despite elevated uncertainty that owes, in part, to the conflict in the Middle East. Productivity growth and capital investment are strong. Job gains have kept pace with the workforce, and the unemployment rate has changed little."
- **资产负债表**：维持 "ample reserves"，暂不缩表（约 6.7 万亿美元）
- **声明长度**：约 130 词（vs 4-29 的 341 词），删除"宽松倾向"前瞻指引措辞
